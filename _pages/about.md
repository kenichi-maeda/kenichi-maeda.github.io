I'm Kenichi Maeda, a Master of Science student in Computer Science at Brown University. My interests span machine learning, computer vision, visualization, medical imaging, and robotics.

My current research focuses on robot manipulation, including dexterous manipulation. I'm interested in imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and multimodal vision-language-action (VLA) models, particularly how to train policies efficiently and transfer them from simulation to robust operation on physical robots.

Previously, I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of Massachusetts Boston, where I worked on multimodal large language models and geometric mesh processing.
